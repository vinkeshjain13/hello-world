Problem statement

Quarterly Regulatory Filing · Problem one-pager, Day 1

Who

Regulatory reporting analysts who prepare the quarterly filing

Pain

Manual, deadline-pressured checking. A missed check or broken trace becomes a material misstatement.

Cost & frequency

Every quarter · 12 days of preparer + reviewer effort per cycle

Our one-sentence problem statement

Our regulatory reporting analysts spend 12 days each quarter checking and tracing the quarterly filing because the numbers span many source feeds and every cell must tie to its source; an agent could pre-run the edit checks and trace each filed value back to its source row, flagging failures for a human to resolve and sign off before filing, using the RWA/capital feeds and the regulatory reporting instructions, cutting the check-and-trace effort to a focused review.

Hard constraint:

A human must sign off before filing. AI may pre-validate and trace, but cannot autonomously file.

Current tools and systems

  • RWA feeds (credit, market, op-risk)
  • Capital / HC-R source rows
  • Schedule and cell workbook (RC-R, HC-R)
  • Regulatory reporting instructions
